🔬 Sleep Science
The body releases ~70% of daily growth hormone during the first deep-sleep cycle. This cellular repair peaks between 10 PM – 2 AM. After 2 AM, REM takes over for memory & emotion. Aligning bedtime with natural melatonin rise (~9–10 PM) and waking with the cortisol surge (6–7 AM) maximises full recovery.
